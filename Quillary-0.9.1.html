Quillary 0.9.1

The phrases Quillary reads out of a sentence are now marked in the card. In the title each one lights up once it settles and stays in the color of the list you are capturing into. In the notes it is underlined instead.

Six parser fixes came out of building it:

  • Editing a url saved the old address
  • due before midnight and by noon set no date
  • beginning of next week resolved to seven days out, not Monday
  • monday morning - at 10 and Breakfast - 10am came back as 9:00
  • Dinner tomorrow - 7pm saved with the whole sentence as its title
  • Emoji in a title were stripped

Requires macOS 26 or later.
