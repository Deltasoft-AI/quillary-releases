Quillary 0.9.1

As you type a reminder, Quillary reads the date, time, repeat, priority and alarm out of the sentence. This release highlights the words it used, in the color of the list you are capturing into, so you can see the reminder was understood the way you meant it.

This release also fixes:

  • Editing a link no longer saves the previous address
  • due before midnight and by noon now set a time
  • beginning of next week now resolves to the coming Monday
  • The time you write is respected in phrases such as monday morning - at 10
  • Dinner tomorrow - 7pm keeps its subject in the title
  • Emoji are no longer removed from titles

Requires macOS 26 or later.
