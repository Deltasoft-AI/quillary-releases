0.9 — first beta

First public build. Feature-complete for daily use, but not yet widely tested. Please report what breaks.

  • Global hotkey capture over any app, straight into Apple Reminders
  • Plain-language dates: every weekday, by eod, the Tuesday after next at 2
  • Priorities, custom alerts and repeats, read from the sentence
  • Recurring habits with streak tracking

Requires macOS 26 or later.
