Quillary 0.9

The first public release of Quillary, a quick-capture panel for Apple Reminders. A global hotkey opens a capture card over whatever you are working in, and you write the reminder the way you would say it out loud.

Quillary reads the details from the sentence as you type:

  • Dates and times, from tomorrow at 5pm to the Tuesday after next at 2
  • Repeats, such as every weekday
  • Priorities and custom alerts, read from the sentence
  • Recurring reminders with streak tracking
  • Filing into a different list for each capture

Requires macOS 26 or later.
